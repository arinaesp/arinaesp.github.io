background

Teacher, then engineer

Before writing code, I was teaching English — IELTS Writing and Speaking, IDP-certified, with additional TEFL/TESOL training. I taught adults relocating abroad, professionals moving into IT careers, school-age kids, and teenagers preparing for standardized tests. I wrote full course curricula from scratch rather than teaching from someone else's material.

When I later started teaching kids to code, I noticed the two skills reinforced each other far more than teaching them separately — which became the founding idea behind axo. You can read the full research behind that on the axo research page.
