CT-DGBLL (formerly ILLA)

ILLA (Integrated Language-Logic Acquisition) was the working name, in 2026, of the framework now called CT-DGBLL: Computational Thinking Digital Game-Based Language Learning.

It describes how young learners who cannot yet read English can learn early English literacy and computational thinking at the same time, through commands that are English words and program instructions at once.

Two conceptual papers are in preparation. Links will appear here when they are available. axo is the app that implements the framework.

The earlier version of this page described ILLA and is superseded.
